Cookie policy

Last updated: July 2026. This page explains what cookies we use on SafeOnline UK and why.

The short version: We only use cookies that are strictly necessary for the website to work. We do not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies that share data with third parties, or any cookies that track you across other websites. You do not need to give consent for essential cookies under UK law.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, and to provide information to the site owner.

Cookies can be "session cookies" (which are deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (which remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them manually).

Under UK law (UK GDPR and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 — PECR), website operators must tell you about cookies and, for non-essential cookies, obtain your consent before setting them. Because we only use essential cookies, we do not require your consent — but we still explain what we use and why.

2. Cookies we use

SafeOnline UK uses only strictly necessary (essential) cookies. These cookies are required for the website to function correctly and cannot be switched off.

Cookie name Provider Purpose Type Duration
sol-cookie-notice SafeOnline UK Records that you have dismissed the cookie notice, so it is not shown on every page visit. Essential 1 year
Session storage (not a cookie) SafeOnline UK The Family Safety Check and Tech Contract Builder store your in-progress answers in your browser's session storage only. This data never leaves your device and is cleared when you close your browser tab. Essential Session only

We do not currently use any analytics platform (such as Google Analytics) and do not set any analytics cookies. If this changes, this policy will be updated and the cookie notice on the site will be updated accordingly.

3. Cookies we do not use

We do not use any of the following:

  • Advertising or targeting cookies — we do not serve adverts and do not use cookies to build a profile of you for advertising purposes
  • Social media tracking cookies — we link to external sites but do not embed social media widgets that set cookies
  • Third-party analytics cookies — we do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, Hotjar, or similar tools
  • Cross-site tracking — we do not track your activity across other websites
  • Profiling or personalisation cookies — we do not build a profile of you or personalise content based on prior behaviour

4. Third-party cookies

Netlify (our hosting provider)

Our website is hosted by Netlify. When you submit a contact form or email signup, Netlify processes that submission. Netlify may set its own cookies as part of its infrastructure operation. These are technical/operational cookies rather than tracking cookies. See Netlify's privacy policy for details.

Tabler Icons CDN (jsdelivr.net)

We load icon fonts from the jsDelivr CDN (jsdelivr.net). When your browser requests these files, jsDelivr may log your IP address. jsDelivr does not set advertising cookies. See jsDelivr's privacy policy.

External links

Our site links to external organisations including Childline, CEOP, NSPCC, Ofcom, and others. When you click through to their websites, those organisations' own cookie policies apply. We have no control over and are not responsible for cookies set by external sites.

5. Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Instructions for the most common browsers are below:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data

Please be aware that deleting or blocking the essential cookie that records your notice dismissal will cause the cookie notice to reappear on your next visit. Blocking all cookies may affect the interactive tools on this site (Family Safety Check, Tech Contract Builder) as they use session storage.

More information on managing cookies: allaboutcookies.org and the ICO's guide to cookies.

6. Changes to this policy

We will update this cookie policy if we change the cookies we use. If we introduce any non-essential cookies (for example, analytics tools), we will update this policy and the site's cookie notice to seek your consent before setting those cookies.

The date at the top of this page shows when the policy was last reviewed.

7. Contact us

If you have any questions about how we use cookies, please contact us via our contact form.

For complaints about how we handle your data more broadly, you can also contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
