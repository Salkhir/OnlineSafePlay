Cookie policy

Last updated: July 2026. This page explains exactly what cookies SafeOnline UK uses, why we use them, and how you can control them.

The short version: We only use cookies that are strictly necessary for the website to work. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, analytics cookies that report to third parties, or any cookies that follow you across other websites. Because we only use essential cookies, we do not legally need your consent to set them — but we still explain everything openly on this page.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit. They are used for a wide range of purposes — from keeping you logged in, to remembering your preferences, to tracking your behaviour across the web for advertising purposes.

Not all cookies are the same. The law distinguishes between:

  • Strictly necessary (essential) cookies — required for a website to function. These cannot be switched off. No consent is required for these under UK law.
  • Functional cookies — remember your preferences (e.g. language or region). Consent is required.
  • Analytics cookies — measure how visitors use a site (e.g. Google Analytics). Consent is required if data is shared with third parties.
  • Advertising / targeting cookies — used to build a profile of you and show relevant ads. Consent is always required.
UK law on cookies: The Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR), read alongside the UK GDPR, govern how websites must use cookies. Strictly necessary cookies are exempt from the consent requirement. All other types require clear, informed consent before being set.

SafeOnline UK only uses strictly necessary cookies. We do not use any other category.

2. Cookies we use

We set only one first-party cookie on this site. It is strictly necessary and records your preference when you dismiss the cookie notice, so it does not reappear on every page.

Cookie name Set by Purpose Category Duration Data stored
sol-cookie-notice SafeOnline UK Records that you have dismissed the cookie notice banner, so it is not displayed again on future visits. Essential 1 year, then automatically deleted The value 1 — no personal information
Note on session storage: Our interactive tools — the Family Safety Check and the Family Tech Contract Builder — store your in-progress answers in your browser's session storage, not in a cookie. Session storage is only accessible on your device, is never sent to our servers, and is automatically cleared when you close the browser tab. It contains nothing that identifies you personally.

We do not currently use any analytics platform. If we introduce analytics in future, this policy will be updated and the cookie notice on the site will be updated to seek your consent before any analytics cookies are set.

3. What we do not use

We want to be completely transparent about what we have deliberately chosen not to use on this site:

🚫

Advertising cookies

We do not run adverts. We do not use cookies to build a profile of you for advertising or retargeting on other websites.

🚫

Third-party analytics

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, Hotjar, Mixpanel, or any similar tool that sends your data to a third party.

🚫

Social media trackers

We do not embed social media widgets (Like buttons, share buttons, feeds) that set tracking cookies from platforms like Facebook, X, or TikTok.

🚫

Cross-site tracking

We do not track your browsing activity on other websites or share any data with advertising networks or data brokers.

🚫

Personalisation cookies

We do not use cookies to build a profile of you or personalise site content based on your past behaviour.

🚫

Children's data

In line with the ICO's Children's Code, we do not knowingly collect data from under-13s. Our kids' pages set no cookies at all.

4. Third-party services

Some parts of this site rely on trusted third-party services to function. These services may set their own cookies or log certain data. We have selected providers who operate with privacy as a priority.

Hosting provider

Netlify

SafeOnline UK is hosted by Netlify, Inc. (San Francisco, USA). Netlify processes form submissions — including our contact form and email alert sign-up — on our behalf. As part of its infrastructure operation, Netlify may set its own technical cookies or log your IP address for security and performance purposes. These are operational rather than tracking cookies.

Netlify complies with UK GDPR via Standard Contractual Clauses for international data transfers. See Netlify's privacy policy for full details.

CDN

We load icon fonts from the jsDelivr content delivery network (jsdelivr.net). When your browser requests these files, jsDelivr may log your IP address as standard CDN access logging. jsDelivr does not set advertising cookies or use this data for tracking. See jsDelivr's privacy policy.

External links

Links to external organisations

Our site links to external organisations including Childline, CEOP, the NSPCC, Ofcom, the ICO, and others. When you follow a link to any external website, that organisation's own cookie and privacy policies apply. We are not responsible for, and have no control over, cookies set by third-party websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy and cookie policies of any external website you visit, including those we link to.

5. Your rights

Under UK GDPR and PECR, you have rights in relation to how your data is handled, including data stored in cookies. Because our one cookie stores no personal information (just the value 1), most of these rights have limited practical application here — but they apply in full to our broader data practices described in our privacy policy.

Right to be informedThis cookie policy fulfils our obligation to tell you clearly what cookies we use and why.
Right to objectYou can block or delete cookies at any time using your browser settings (see section 6 below).
Right of accessYou can request details of any personal data we hold about you via our contact form.
Right to erasureYou can ask us to delete any personal data we hold. Contact us and we will respond within one month.
Right to complainIf you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.
Withdraw consentAlthough our cookies do not require consent, you can delete our cookie at any time by clearing cookies in your browser.

6. Managing and deleting cookies

You can view, manage, and delete cookies stored on your device at any time through your browser's settings. The steps vary by browser:

Google Chrome

Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data → See all site data and permissions

Mozilla Firefox

Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Manage Data

Apple Safari

Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data → search for onlinesafeplay.com

Microsoft Edge

Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data → See all cookies and site data

Samsung Internet

Settings → Privacy and security → Delete browsing data → Cookies and site data

Opera

Settings → Advanced → Privacy & security → Site Settings → Cookies and site data

What happens if you delete our cookie? If you delete the sol-cookie-notice cookie, the cookie notice banner will reappear on your next visit to the site. This is because the banner has no way of knowing you have previously dismissed it. Simply click "Got it" again to dismiss it.

Blocking all cookies in your browser may affect some interactive features on this site. In particular, the Family Safety Check and Family Tech Contract Builder use browser session storage (not cookies) to hold your answers temporarily — blocking all storage may prevent these tools from working correctly.

For more general guidance on managing cookies, see:

  • allaboutcookies.org — independent guide to cookies and browser settings
  • ICO guide to cookies — the UK regulator's public guidance

7. Changes to this policy

We will update this cookie policy if our use of cookies changes. If we introduce any non-essential cookies — for example, an analytics tool to understand how visitors use the site — we will update this policy and the cookie notice banner to seek your explicit consent before setting those cookies.

Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We will not make changes that reduce your privacy protections without clearly informing you.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions about this cookie policy or how we handle your data more broadly, please contact us using our contact form. We aim to respond to all data-related enquiries within five working days.

For unresolved complaints about data protection, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) — the UK's independent data protection regulator:

  • Website: ico.org.uk
  • Helpline: 0303 123 1113 (Monday–Friday, 9am–5pm)
  • Post: Information Commissioner's Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire SK9 5AF
